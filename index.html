Selected Work

Each project notes not just the tool, but the reasoning behind the design decision — the judgement that turns software output into sound engineering.

QGIS · Civil 3D

Regional Road Upgrade — GIS-to-BIM Corridor

2022 · 40 km arterial

Integrated QGIS terrain and slope analysis directly into a Civil 3D corridor model to optimise alignment against cut/fill and flood exposure.

ReasoningChose a 0.6% ruling grade over the steeper minimum-length option because the DEM showed a saturated sub-base zone — trading 1.2 km of length for long-term drainage stability.

ArcGIS Pro

Renewable-Energy Siting — Piedmont

2020 · Multi-criteria analysis

Weighted-overlay suitability model across slope, aspect, grid distance and land-use constraints to rank candidate sites for a utility client.

ReasoningDown-weighted pure irradiance in favour of grid-connection cost, because interconnection — not sunlight — was the binding economic constraint.

Revit · FreeCAD IFC

Municipal Utility Coordination — IFC Federation

2019 · 12 municipalities

Federated water, gas and telecom models into a single IFC environment for clash detection and openBIM hand-over to public authorities.

ReasoningEnforced an IFC-first (not Revit-native) exchange so the asset would survive the client's mixed-vendor software estate for decades.

Rhino 3D · Inventor

Pedestrian Bridge — Parametric Form Study

2017 · Campus retrofit

Parametric exploration of a steel footbridge in Rhino, with connection details engineered in Inventor for fabrication.

ReasoningCapped the arch rise to keep deck gradient within accessibility limits — letting the constraint, not aesthetics, set the geometry.
